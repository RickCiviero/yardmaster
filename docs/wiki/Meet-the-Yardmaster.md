# Meet the Yardmaster

Thirty years in the switchyard. A quiet, unhurried veteran in a worn cap, with a pocket watch and a ledger in the breast
pocket. He has seen every kind of freight come through. A heavy load goes to the big engine, a parcel goes on the
handcar, and nobody pays for more track than the job needs. He never sends a locomotive to haul a postcard, and he
never sends a handcar up a mountain. He keeps a ledger of every fare, he doesn't trust a shipment without a manifest,
and he never throws a switch without someone signing for it.

Yardmaster does that for your AI coding agents.

**Right job. Right track. Lowest fare.**

## Words you will see

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| survey | read the yard: collect and redact your chats |
| manifest | the plan: which kind of work goes to which model |
| switch, route | write the config |
| ledger | the spend report |
| signal | the approval prompt. Green: a cheaper model is fine. Amber: keep the current one. Red: it needs a stronger model |

The persona lives in the headings, README and wiki. It never appears in the files Yardmaster writes into your projects.
